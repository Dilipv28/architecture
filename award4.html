The Challenge of
Permanence.

Commissioned in 2019, the Net-Zero Housing initiative was designed to challenge the obsolescence of modern building materials. Our goal was to create a structure that didn't just "do no harm," but actively contributed to its local micro-climate.

Utilizing a combination of Cross-Laminated Timber (CLT) and photobioreactive algae facades, the building achieves thermal mass efficiency that exceeds standard insulation by 40%. The result is a residential complex that breathes, evolving with the seasons.

Passive Solar Orientation

Optimized building geometry to maximize winter solar gain while providing 100% natural shading during peak summer hours.

Circular Materials

90% of the interior finishes are derived from mycelium-based composites and reclaimed industrial timber.

Smart-Grid Integration

Excess energy generated by the rooftop solar kinetic array is redistributed back to the municipal grid, powering 15 neighboring homes.

Biodiversity Roof

A 500sqm rooftop garden designed to support local pollinator species and reduce the urban heat island effect.
