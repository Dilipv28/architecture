Common Queries

Do you provide end-to-end site visits?

Yes. We organize physical site visits including technical surveys for topography, soil testing, and local neighborhood analysis before you sign any documents.

How does legal vetting work for plots?

Our in-house legal team performs a 30-year title search, verifies EC (Encumbrance Certificate), and ensures the land is free from any litigation or government acquisition notices.

Are your construction services customizable?

Absolutely. Our architectural wing provides bespoke 3D designs tailored to your budget, ensuring modern aesthetics with maximum floor area utilization.
