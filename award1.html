Architectural Symbiosis

The Vertical Forest HQ serves as a prototype for the future of urban architecture. By hosting over 20,000 plants and trees across its facade, the structure actively filters CO2 and regulates micro-climates.

This project was honored with the AIA Innovation Award for its breakthrough in gravity-defying irrigation systems and bio-diverse facade engineering.
