Brooklyn, New York

Brooklyn Commons

Brooklyn Commons represents a radical shift in urban commercial planning. Spanning six city blocks, the project bridges the gap between private enterprise and public utility.

Our approach utilized "Tactical Urbanism"—creating fluid transitions between indoor lobbies and outdoor plazas, encouraging a 24/7 ecosystem of activity.
